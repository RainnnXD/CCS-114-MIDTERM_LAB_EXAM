About Me

Hello, I'm Rainzell — a student, photographer, and a future developer based in Cabuyao, Laguna.

I specialize in portrait, wedding, street, and landscape photography. I love chasing real moments, capturing genuine emotions, candid interactions, and the little details that make each story special. For me, photography is about freezing those moments in time and turning them into memories you can always come back to.

Beyond photography, I’m also passionate about technology and aspire to become a future developer. I enjoy learning how technology works, building creative solutions, and exploring how coding can turn ideas into something useful and meaningful. I hope to combine my creativity from photography with my technical skills in development, creating work that is both visually engaging and purposeful.

Mission & Vision

Mission & Vision

As a student of the University of Cabuyao (Pamantasan ng Cabuyao), here is the institution's guiding Mission and Vision.

Vision

A premier educational instituion of higher learning in Region IV, developing globally-competitive and value-laden professionals and leaders instrumental to community development and nation building.

Mission

As institution of higher learning, University of Cabuyao (PnC) is committed to equip individuals with the knowledge, skills, and values that will enable them to achieve their professional goals, and provide leadership and service for national development.

Pamantasan ng Cabuyao commits to adhering to statutory and regulatory requirements, promoting high levels of customer engagement, and maintaining an effective quality management system through periodic review and communication of quality objectives for continuous improvement of quality services in instruction, research, and extension.

Founded on April 16, 2003, the University of Cabuyao (Pamantasan ng Cabuyao) is a locally-funded university in Banay-Banay, Cabuyao, Laguna, offering programs in fields such as information technology, computer science, business, education, engineering, and health sciences.
